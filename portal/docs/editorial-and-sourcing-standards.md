**Status:** Proposed working standard, version 1.0. **Research date:** 7 November 2026.

New Wow Fashion prompts and operational guidelines authored for the rebuild, informed by the live brand context and the user-supplied Writing Room and affiliate reference portals. Shared writing guidance was found in the reference portals; no existing Wow Fashion-specific internal prompt pack was supplied or located. ## Editorial standard

### Brand position

Accessible women’s fashion, explained with practical detail.

- Use Wow Fashion in editorial copy; preserve existing category URL slugs until the migration plan changes them.
- Current public positioning emphasizes women’s clothing, accessible prices and supplier selection. Treat this as source-site positioning, not proof of current operations.
- Keep the seven existing department families as the starting taxonomy. Do not invent new live categories or products.
- US English is the proposed default because the existing taxonomy uses pants and US-oriented language. Make market, currency and destination configurable.

### Voice and language

Friendly, style-aware and specific without inflated promises.

- Use natural contractions, sentence case headings, varied sentence lengths and parallel bullets. Follow the shared reference guidelines on straight quotes and no em/en dashes.
- Describe the silhouette, construction, use or buying decision rather than repeating generic enthusiasm.
- Avoid staged contrast formulas, repetitive three-part slogans, fake urgency, repeated superlatives, keyword stuffing and canned phrases such as “elevate your style”.
- Use inclusive language. Do not imply a body needs correcting, promise a universal flattering fit, or impose age-based wardrobe rules.
- Style advice can express a considered preference; factual product claims need evidence.

### Evidence and honest recommendations

Separate what a seller says, what reviewers report and what Wow Fashion tested.

- Maintain a source ledger with URL, date, selected variant, destination and the fact it supports.
- Never invent ratings, reviews, sales, quotes, test results, certifications or manufacturer details.
- Use first-person testing language only when a documented physical test supports that exact claim.
- Explain the limits of desk research; a product score is an internal comparison aid, not a quality guarantee.
- Keep unverified facts out of promotional copy and make them visible in internal review notes.

### Materials and performance claims

Composition and performance must come from appropriate evidence.

- Distinguish fiber composition, weave and finish: satin does not automatically mean silk; a silk-themed category does not prove silk content.
- Differentiate leather and faux leather, wool and wool blends, and cashmere claims with precise supporting specifications.
- Do not infer stretch, opacity, warmth, water resistance, breathability or durability solely from photographs or style names.
- Do not claim sustainable, ethical, organic, recycled or certified without evidence that applies to the product and claim.
- Use verified care instructions or label evidence. Do not invent washing temperatures or dry-cleaning requirements.

### Sizing and fit

Use the exact chart for the exact garment.

- State whether the chart gives body measurements or garment measurements. Do not guess when the source is unclear.
- Preserve units, ranges and tolerances. Use 2.54 cm per inch for conversions and avoid false precision.
- Never assume S/M/L labels or regional size numbers are consistent across stores and products.
- Summarize fit feedback with its sample size and variant limitations; do not make blanket sizing-up claims.
- Keep material or measurement contradictions visible until resolved.

### Commercial model and policy boundaries

Every page must know who sells, ships and handles returns.

- Each page brief must select affiliate, direct retail or undecided mode.
- Affiliate pages identify the retailer and use an outbound CTA; do not imply Wow Fashion handles the order.
- Retail pages use only the current approved Wow Fashion price, stock and customer policies.
- Legacy shipping, refund and item-testing claims require fresh operational confirmation before reuse.
- Capture the selected variant, destination, currency, shipping/tax treatment and check date with every displayed price.
- Do not hard-code a universal AliExpress return window. Verify current item, market and order terms.

### Affiliate disclosure and links

Make the commercial relationship clear where readers encounter it.

- Use clear commission disclosure near the recommendation and before the first affiliate link; apply the approved wording for the target market.
- For UK-directed affiliate content, use clear advertising identification for the relevant content and links; do not rely on a hidden footer or vague “may earn” language.
- Qualify affiliate links with rel="sponsored" in the implementation.
- Do not invent tracking URLs, commission terms or affiliate-program membership.
- Rank products on reader value and evidence, not commission.

### Search and page structure

Make the page’s purpose clear and give it original value.

- Use one clear H1, useful subheadings, unique metadata and descriptive link text.
- Keep top-level categories, subcategories, products, guides and filters distinct.
- Treat title/description character counts as drafting guides, not guarantees of display or performance.
- Preserve existing URLs unless an approved migration map specifies an equivalent new destination.
- Do not invent FAQ answers, schema ratings, keyword metrics or performance data.
- Use word ranges to manage scope, not to pad copy or meet a supposed ranking requirement.

### Supplier verification

A seller record and a verified manufacturer are different things.

- Store the platform store ID and URL so similarly named suppliers are not merged by mistake.
- Keep business type, verification status and commercial status as separate fields.
- A marketplace badge, factory-themed name or high rating does not prove a manufacturer relationship.
- Record sample findings, product scope, reviewer and approval date before using approved status.
- Retain business contact channels only; do not store account passwords, private identification documents or payment details in this portal.

### Editorial workflow and freshness

Research, draft, check and review with a visible record.

- Recommended stages: planned, researching, brief ready, drafting, review, approved, published, refresh due.
- Product candidate stages and supplier stages are separate from page-writing stages.
- Refresh price, availability and destination-specific terms at publication; the proposed sourcing evidence window is 14 days.
- Assign an owner, evidence date, next review date and source record to each meaningful decision.
- A prompt creates a draft or analysis. It does not automatically publish, order samples, contact suppliers or approve vendors.

## Product scoring

Prioritize comparable AliExpress candidates for human and sample review. This is a house rubric, not an AliExpress standard or a prediction of product quality.

These weights, priors and thresholds are proposed internal choices. They are not published AliExpress criteria and have not been validated against actual Wow Fashion return or quality outcomes. Calibrate them after reviewing samples.

| Component | Weight | Method |
|---|---:|---|
| Review-adjusted rating | 30 | adjustedRating = (rating * ratingCount + 4.2 * 50) / (ratingCount + 50); points = 30 * clamp((adjustedRating - 3) / 2, 0, 1) |
| Sold-volume signal | 15 | points = 15 * min(log10(displayedSoldLowerBound + 1) / log10(5001), 1) |
| Recent review evidence | 15 | points = 15 * grade / 5 |
| Seller evidence | 15 | points = 15 * grade / 5 |
| Fit and specification clarity | 10 | points = 10 * grade / 5 |
| Comparable landed value | 10 | points = 10 * grade / 5 |
| Delivery and returns clarity | 5 | points = 5 * grade / 5 |

### How the rating adjustment works

Use r for the displayed product rating and n for the number of rating responses supporting it. The prior of 4.2 with a strength of 50 responses deliberately pulls tiny samples toward a cautious baseline. It is a house assumption, not a claim about the average AliExpress rating.

`Adjusted rating = (r × n + 4.2 × 50) / (n + 50)`

`Rating points = 30 × clamp((adjusted rating − 3) / 2, 0, 1)`

A hypothetical 4.9-star item with four rating responses has an adjusted rating of approximately 4.252 and earns about 18.78/30. A hypothetical 4.7-star item with 500 responses has an adjusted rating of approximately 4.655 and earns about 24.82/30. These are illustrative calculations, not real product findings.

### Demand signal

`Demand points = 15 × min(log10(sold lower bound + 1) / log10(5,001), 1)`

Preserve a displayed “1,000+” as a lower bound of 1,000 with the original label. Keep listing-wide versus variant-specific scope visible. Do not use the ratio of reviews to sales as a fraud test.

### Qualitative grades

Each qualitative component uses a documented grade from 0 to 5. The contribution is its weight multiplied by grade/5. Unknown is null, not 0. The JSON contains exact anchors for every grade.

### Incomplete evidence

Show earned points / 100, evaluated weight / 100 and possible ceiling = earned points + unevaluated weight. Label any partial result Incomplete score. Never rescale known points to a 100-point total.

Any unknown critical gate or unscored component means More evidence needed, regardless of earned points.

For example, 54 earned points with 70/100 weight evaluated is an incomplete 54/100 with a possible ceiling of 84/100. It must not be displayed as 77.1/100 by normalizing away the unknown components.

### Gates and decisions

- **Correct item and variant available:** Live exact item/store/variant is verified. If unknown: More evidence needed.
- **Target destination supported:** Current destination availability and cost/estimate are recorded. If unknown: More evidence needed.
- **Identity and authenticity concerns resolved:** Store/item identity is clear and no unresolved substantiated authenticity concern is recorded. If unknown: Hold for verification; do not publish an accusation.
- **Essential material and sizing evidence:** Required composition and product-specific size information are coherent. If unknown: Hold until essential gaps are resolved.
- **Serious recurring issues checked:** Recent sample and available records contain no unresolved repeated serious issue identified by the reviewer. If unknown: More evidence needed; no reviews is not proof of no defects.
- **Evidence current enough for this decision:** Key listing/seller evidence is within 14 days, with prices and availability rechecked at publication. If unknown: Record dates before a decision.

| Decision | Condition |
|---|---|
| Do not shortlist | A critical gate demonstrably fails, or a zero grade records a critical concern in recent reviews, seller, specifications or delivery. Record the evidence and reason. |
| More evidence needed | A critical gate is unknown, evidence is stale, or any scoring component is unscored. |
| Pilot only | Gates and evidence are complete, but observed rating < 4.5, rating count < 50, or sold lower bound < 100. Any exception requires a documented sample and reviewer decision. |
| Priority shortlist | All gates pass, history thresholds met, evidence complete and score >= 80. Human/sample review still required. |
| Compare alternatives | All gates pass, history thresholds met, evidence complete and 65 <= score < 80. |
| Deprioritize | All gates pass, history thresholds met, evidence complete and score < 65. |

### Evidence confidence

Evidence confidence is a descriptive research label, not a statistical probability that the product is good.

- **High:** Complete current evidence, at least 200 rating responses, at least 10 substantive relevant recent reviews, and selected-variant scope is clear.
- **Medium:** Complete current evidence, at least 50 rating responses and five substantive relevant recent reviews, with scope limitations disclosed.
- **Low:** Incomplete, stale, sparse or mismatched evidence; a higher earned score does not override this label.

## Supplier directory

Start with an empty directory; add real researched suppliers. No illustrative company should look like an approved supplier.

Business type, verification status and commercial status must be separate. A store is not a verified manufacturer, and supplier approval applies only to its recorded scope. The schema includes stable store IDs, category coverage, public business contact, linked candidate products, sample records, media permissions, evidence dates, reviewer and next review.

- An Approved record requires reviewer, date and scope.
- Manufacturer type needs an evidence note; otherwise keep Unknown or the verified retailer/trader type.
- Keep private credentials, payment details and identity documents outside this portal.
- A supplier status never silently approves all of its products.
