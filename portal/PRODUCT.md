# Wow Fashion Portal

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Implementation choice: a dependency-light JavaScript web application with Vercel Node functions, private Blob storage, and a GitHub source repository. Hosting is explicitly requested on Vercel.

## Users
Wow Fashion's website team: people planning the rebuild, researching products and suppliers, and preparing category and product copy.

## Product Purpose
A shared workspace that joins the existing website map to product curation, supplier evidence and reusable writing prompts.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Current category URLs and hierarchy are evidence, not a proposed migration plan. Shop taxonomy and editorial landing pages remain distinct. The user explicitly requested AliExpress as the primary sourcing platform. New writing guidelines and the scoring rubric are proposed working standards. A seller is not a verified manufacturer without evidence. Real workspace records require authentication and persist in private storage. No real products or suppliers have been represented as vetted.

## Brand Commitments
Follow the practical page-led workflows in the user's four reference portals. Preserve Wow Fashion's women's fashion subject and current category naming in source records. Use clear, direct writing without em dashes.

## Open Decisions
Business model (affiliate, direct retail or hybrid), target market, final taxonomy/URL migration plan and operating policies require business confirmation. The workspace can record these decisions without blocking research.
