BEFORE THE NIGHT BEGINS

A few little details.

How do I choose my size?+

Open your favourite dress in the WOW Fashion shop and check its individual measurements and fabric details. Compare those measurements with a dress you already love wearing.

Where can I buy these dresses?+

Every product card takes you to its matching WOW Fashion product page, where you can choose available options and complete your purchase. The hero is campaign inspiration; the collection photographs show the shop’s products.

What about delivery and returns?+

Check the latest delivery options and return conditions in the WOW Fashion shop before ordering. Availability and delivery charges are confirmed by the store at checkout.

Can I save my favourites?+

Absolutely. Tap the heart on a dress to add it to your saved night edit. Your favourites stay in this browser on this device; saving does not reserve an item.
