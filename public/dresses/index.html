A LITTLE HELP, BEFORE YOU FALL IN LOVE

Good questions.
Better choices.

Choosing a dress should feel exciting. Here’s where to start, from finding a silhouette to checking the details that matter.

Explore the shop ↗
How do I find the right dress for my plans?+

Explore the dress categories above. For an evening out, explore cocktail and evening styles; for slower days, look at casual or boho dresses. A length can be a useful starting point too. Browse short, long or tea-length collections, then consider the dress code, weather and how you want to feel.

Where can I check sizing and fabric details?+

Open the dress’s product page on WOW Fashion before choosing a size. Check that individual item’s measurements, material description and available options rather than assuming every style fits the same. Compare the listed measurements with a similar garment you already enjoy wearing.

Can I buy the dresses shown here?+

Yes. The dress cards link to the existing WOW Fashion shop, where you can check current pricing, available colours and sizes, and complete your purchase. The campaign film is styling inspiration; use the product photographs and descriptions to see the actual pieces offered for sale.

How does my saved edit work?+

Use the heart beside a dress to add or remove it from your edit. Your choices are stored in this browser on this device, with no account needed. Saving a dress doesn’t reserve stock or add it to a shopping bag. Open a saved item to see its latest details in the shop.

Where do I check delivery and returns?+

Review the store’s current delivery and returns information before placing an order. Delivery options, charges and applicable return conditions should be confirmed on WOW Fashion and during checkout. This inspiration page does not promise a delivery date or change the store’s policies.
